How loud is the noise?

A detector's noise is described by its power spectral density: how much the data wiggles at each frequency. To estimate it, the data is cut into segments, each segment's spectrum is taken, and the spectra are averaged. That is Welch's method, and it runs here in a background thread every time you change a setting.

Each segment xk is multiplied by a Hann window w, transformed, and squared; the results are averaged over the K segments and scaled so the area under the curve is the variance of the data.
