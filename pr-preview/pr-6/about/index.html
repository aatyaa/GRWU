About GRWU

GRWU is a set of interactive essays on gravitational-wave data analysis. Each one takes a single idea, tells the story of how it came to be needed, and lets you move the figures until the idea is yours.

Two ways to read

Every essay is written to be read as a story, with figures you can drag, scrub and replay. Underneath each idea is its mathematical layer: the claim, what may be assumed, the derivation with the step it turns on, and what it means. Open one where you want it, or all of them at once with the control at the top of an essay.

What the colours mean

Colour is never decoration here. Amber is the signal, and anything observed or measured. Blue-grey is noise, models and schematics. Red is bias and disagreement, and it is always labelled. Every figure also says what kind of picture it is: measured, model, schematic, or a control whose answer is known in advance.

Data

Strain data comes from the Gravitational Wave Open Science Center, which publishes data from the LIGO, Virgo and KAGRA observatories. It is analysed in your browser.
